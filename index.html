The Illusions Concept

One address.
Two transformations.

Tucked on Wisconsin Avenue in the heart of Georgetown, Illusions GT Auto Spa shares its home with Illusions of Georgetown — a full-service salon. While our team restores your vehicle to a mirror finish downstairs, you can step inside for a cut, color, or treatment of your own.

It is a simple idea done with obsessive care: elite auto detailing and personal styling, delivered with unmatched attention to detail, in a single visit — with complimentary parking while you're with us.
